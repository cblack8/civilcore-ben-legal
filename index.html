CivilCore BEN – Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of CivilCore BEN can request deletion of their account and/or personal data. This process applies to all users who have created an account within the app using email and password authentication.

Request Full Account Deletion

To permanently delete your CivilCore BEN account and all associated data, please send an email to:

Email: support@civilcoreben.com

Your deletion request must include:

  • The email address associated with your CivilCore BEN account
  • A clear statement requesting account deletion

Once your request is received, your account and all associated data will be deleted within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

What Data Is Deleted

The following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Account email
  • Authentication credentials
  • User profile information
  • App usage data stored in Supabase

What Data May Be Retained

Some non‑personal, aggregated, or anonymized data may be retained for security, fraud prevention, or analytics purposes. This data cannot be used to identify you.

Request Partial Data Deletion (Without Deleting Your Account)

If you prefer to delete specific pieces of data while keeping your account active, you may request partial deletion by emailing:

Email: support@civilcoreben.com

You may request deletion of:

  • Profile information
  • Saved preferences
  • Stored usage data

Partial deletion requests are processed within 14 days.

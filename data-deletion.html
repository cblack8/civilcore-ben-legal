User Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Types of data we collect

CivilCore BEN may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Usage analytics (feature usage, performance, interaction data)

Requesting data deletion

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at chrisss3219@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”.

What we delete

Upon a valid data deletion request, we will:

  • Remove or anonymize personal identifiers (such as name and email)
  • Remove or anonymize usage analytics data where reasonably possible
  • Retain only data required by law (e.g., payment or tax records)

Timeframe

We aim to process data deletion requests within 30 days, subject to technical and legal constraints.

Impact of data deletion

Deleting your data may affect your ability to use certain features, especially those tied to Premium Memberships or accounts.

Contact

For questions about data deletion, contact: chrisss3219@gmail.com.

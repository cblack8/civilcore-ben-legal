User Account Deletion Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Accounts and Premium Memberships

CivilCore BEN does not require an account for basic usage. Accounts are only required for Premium Memberships and certain advanced features.

How users can delete their account

If you have a Premium Membership or an account with CivilCore BEN, you may request deletion of your account by:

  • Using any in-app “Delete Account” or “Manage Account” feature (if available), or
  • Contacting us at chrisss3219@gmail.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request”.

What happens when an account is deleted

When we process an account deletion request:

  • Your account credentials and profile data are removed or anonymized
  • Access to Premium Membership features tied to that account is revoked
  • We may retain limited data as required by law (e.g., transaction records)

Verification

To protect your privacy, we may ask you to verify your identity or confirm ownership of the account before processing deletion.

Contact

For any questions about account deletion, contact: chrisss3219@gmail.com.
